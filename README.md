World Sort adds a simple category menu when you click Singleplayer, making it easier to find the world you want to play.

Choose from four categories:

* Survival
* Hardcore
* Adventure
* Creative

![Menu](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/e44df469e450384f4c250a15b4e4d23d93f92d28.jpeg)

Selecting a category opens your world list and only shows worlds matching that game mode. Want to see everything? Click All Worlds.

The usual world search and buttons remain available, so you can still create, edit and manage your worlds as normal.

Hardcore worlds have their own category and do not appear under Survival. Worlds are filtered by their saved default game mode.

The best part, it is completely client side!
